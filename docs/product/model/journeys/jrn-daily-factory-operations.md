## Intended Outcome

Juan understands what is happening across his repositories, what needs his intervention and what he can do next, and follows the effect without leaving SoFa for routine operations.

## Entry Conditions

The owner has a configured factory and can authenticate. Every repository may have different capabilities and availability.

## Journey Narrative

From desktop or phone, Juan signs in, finds an issue through search, a project or the intervention inbox, reads its context and evidence, requests eligible work or contributes information, and follows the source-confirmed outcome. He can then inspect project signals to decide where to invest effort. This complements JRN-ENGINEERING-EXECUTION; it does not require a full delivery cycle on every visit.

## Variants and Branches

An idea can be explored and explicitly promoted or linked through UC-TRIAGE-BACKLOG. A direct issue link skips portfolio navigation. An issue with no human wait remains accessible. Missing data, stale decisions and uncertain commands remain visible when switching devices or reopening the workspace.

## Completion Conditions

Juan can identify active work, outstanding human decisions, the effect and status of his own actions, and the source and limitations behind a project finding. A visit can end while admitted work continues independently.
