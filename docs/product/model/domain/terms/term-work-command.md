## Definition

An explicit, attributable request by an authorized person to perform a supported action on identified repository work, with enough source identity to distinguish its submission and outcome from other requests.

## Distinguish From

A command is not the issue's open or closed state, its lifecycle stage, an execution run, a work reservation, a published artifact or a human approval. Requesting Propose does not establish that a proposal exists or that its content is approved. An ordinary comment contributes conversation and is not implicitly a continuation command.

## Usage

UC-OPERATE-ISSUE presents and submits it. FR-AUTHORIZED-ENTRY follows its source acceptance and correlated outcome. Uncertain submission means the outcome is not established, rather than that the source did nothing.
