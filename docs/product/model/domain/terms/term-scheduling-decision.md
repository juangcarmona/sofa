## Definition

A recorded source scheduling decision or SoFa recommendation explaining why a work unit was eligible, selected, deferred, or blocked across projects, including eligibility checks, project and issue priority order, and whether priority was sourced or derived from the shared default.

## Distinguish From

A scheduling decision does not change source priority or authorize a human gate.

## Usage

UC-SCHEDULE-WORK observes it for review by ACT-FACTORY-OWNER.
