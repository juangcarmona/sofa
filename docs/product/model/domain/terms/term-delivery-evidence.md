## Definition

A source-linked record supporting a verification, review, integration, artifact, or delivery claim, with observed time and limitation.

## Distinguish From

Telemetry describes operation; delivery evidence supports what happened to a work unit and its resulting artifact.

## Usage

UC-VERIFY-DELIVERY collects it and presents it to ACT-FACTORY-OWNER.
