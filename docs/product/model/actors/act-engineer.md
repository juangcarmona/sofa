## Purpose

Request refinement, implementation or planning for a repository within explicitly granted capabilities.

## Goals

Move a GitHub work item to an attributable outcome within repository policy.

## Responsibilities

Provide scope and decisions within the actor's verified authority and inspect questions or results that need attention.

## Boundaries

Repository capabilities are verified. Engineering authority does not imply policy administration, unlimited execution or permission to merge. Juan may fill this role without introducing a team or an organization.
