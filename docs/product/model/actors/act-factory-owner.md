## Purpose

Juan uses SoFa to select, supervise, and accept work across his repositories.

## Goals

Make small, dependency-aware work progress recoverably from GitHub backlog to verified delivery.

## Responsibilities

Decide product and architecture intent, set priorities, authorize risk profiles and external actions, review evidence, and approve integration or delivery when required.

## Boundaries

This is a personal factory. No team identity, delegated commercial role, or implicit approval is introduced.
