## Purpose

Juan G. Carmona uses his personal software factory to move configured project work from ideas through verified delivery.

## Goals

Configure projects, shape work, supervise execution, and reach the evidence needed to decide or authorize the next step.

## Responsibilities

Define product direction, configure project policies, inspect evidence, and retain authority over risk decisions and any gates required by policy.

## Boundaries

The factory serves Juan personally. He can admit requesters and authorize repository engineers under BR-WORK-AUTHORIZATION without introducing company portfolios, organization identity administration or commercial accounts.
