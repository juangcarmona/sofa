## Purpose

Submit a product request or defect and answer questions about that request.

## Goals

Move a GitHub work item to an attributable outcome within repository policy.

## Responsibilities

Provide scope and decisions within the actor's verified authority and inspect questions or results that need attention.

## Boundaries

May supply information for admitted work, but cannot grant execution, change policy, expand scope or approve integration without separately verified capabilities.
